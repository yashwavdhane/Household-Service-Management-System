# Household Service Management System (HSMS) - User Manual

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the Household Service Management System (HSMS)! This platform connects customers seeking home services with verified, professional service providers. This manual outlines how to use the system depending on your assigned role: Customer, Provider, or Admin.

---

## 2. General Navigation
- **Home Page**: Browse popular service categories, learn how the platform works, and view statistics.
- **Role-based Dashboards**: Once logged in, you will be redirected to a personalized dashboard tailored to your role.
- **Mobile Navigation**: On mobile devices, use the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-right corner to access your navigation links and logout button.

---

## 3. Customer Guide

### 3.1 Registration & Login
- Click **"Book a Service"** or navigate to the Registration page.
- Select the **Customer** role.
- Fill in your Name, Email, Phone, and Password.
- Once registered, log in to access your Customer Dashboard.

### 3.2 Finding a Service Provider
1. **Browse Services**: Navigate to the "Browse Services" page to see available categories (e.g., Plumbing, Cleaning).
2. **Find Providers**: Use the "Find Providers" page to search and filter active professionals in your area.
3. **Provider Profile**: Click on a provider to view their skills, experience, average rating, reviews, and service area.

### 3.3 Booking a Service
1. On a Provider's Profile, click **"Book Now"**.
2. Select your desired Date, Time, and Service Category.
3. Add any specific details or addresses in the "Notes" section.
4. Click **"Confirm Booking"**. Your booking status will initially be **Pending**.

### 3.4 Managing Your Bookings
- **My Bookings**: View all your past and active bookings in your dashboard.
- **Status Tracking**: Track your booking as it changes from `Pending` -> `Accepted` -> `In Progress` -> `Completed`.
- **Cancellation**: You can cancel a booking while it is in the `Pending` state.

### 3.5 Leaving a Review
- Once a booking is marked as **Completed** by the provider, you can leave a star rating (1-5) and a text review.
- Reviews are visible on the Provider's public profile to help other customers.

---

## 4. Provider Guide

### 4.1 Registration & Profile Setup
- Click **"Become a Provider"** on the home page and register as a Provider.
- Navigate to **"My Profile"** to complete your setup.
- Upload a profile picture and specify your:
  - Skills and Years of Experience
  - Service Categories you offer
  - Service Area (e.g., Downtown, North City)
  - Availability toggle (Available / Unavailable)

### 4.2 Handling Booking Requests
- When a customer books you, you will receive a notification, and the request will appear in your **Booking Requests** tab.
- **Accept/Reject**: Review the job notes and date/time. You can Accept or Reject the pending request.
- **In Progress**: Once you begin the job, update the status to `In Progress`.
- **Completion**: After finishing the job, mark it as `Completed`.

### 4.3 Dashboard Analytics
- The Provider Dashboard gives you a quick overview of your Active Jobs, Total Reviews, Average Rating, and Recent Requests.

---

## 5. Admin Guide

### 5.1 Accessing the Admin Panel
*Note: Admin accounts must be granted permissions directly by the system administrators in the database.*
- Log in with your Admin credentials to access the **Admin Control Panel**.

### 5.2 Managing the Platform
- **User Management**: View, search, and manage all registered Customers and Providers.
- **Provider Verification**: Review new Provider profiles and verify them for platform trust.
- **Category Management**: Create, edit, and disable Service Categories. Providers can only select active categories.
- **Booking Oversight**: Monitor all bookings happening across the platform in real-time.
- **Analytics**: View system-wide metrics, including total users, booking volume, and revenue estimates.

---

## 6. Notifications
- A notification bell icon is present in the top navigation bar.
- It displays a red badge when you have unread notifications.
- Notifications are automatically sent for:
  - New booking requests (To Provider)
  - Booking acceptances/rejections (To Customer)
  - Status updates (To Customer)
